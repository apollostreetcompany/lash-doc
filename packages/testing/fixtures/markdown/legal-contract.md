# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into as of the Effective Date set forth on the signature page below by and between **Lash Inc.** ("**Provider**") and the customer identified on the signature page ("**Customer**"). Provider and Customer may each be referred to individually as a "**Party**" and collectively as the "**Parties**".

## 1. Definitions

1.1. "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such Party.

1.2. "**Confidential Information**" means non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other, in any form, that is identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure.

1.3. "**Documentation**" means the user guides, technical specifications, and other materials made generally available by Provider to its customers describing the operation and use of the Services.

1.4. "**Order Form**" means an ordering document signed by both Parties referencing this Agreement and specifying Services purchased, fees, and term.

1.5. "**Services**" means the products and services described in an Order Form.

## 2. Services

### 2.1 Provision

Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement and the applicable Order Form, Provider will provide Customer with access to the Services during the Subscription Term.

### 2.2 Service Levels

Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Services available in accordance with the Service Level Agreement set forth in **Exhibit A**.

### 2.3 Customer Obligations

Customer will:

- maintain the confidentiality of all login credentials;
- use the Services only in compliance with applicable law and the Documentation;
- promptly notify Provider of any unauthorized access; and
- be responsible for the acts and omissions of its Authorized Users.

## 3. Fees and Payment

### 3.1 Fees

Customer will pay all fees specified in each Order Form. All fees are non-refundable except as expressly set forth herein.

### 3.2 Invoicing

Provider will invoice Customer in accordance with the relevant Order Form. Undisputed invoices are due net thirty (30) days from the invoice date.

### 3.3 Taxes

Fees do not include taxes. Customer is responsible for all sales, use, value-added, and similar taxes assessed in connection with the Services, except for taxes based on Provider's net income.

## 4. Confidentiality

### 4.1 Obligations

Each Party will protect the other Party's Confidential Information using the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar importance, but in no event less than reasonable care.

### 4.2 Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that:

1. is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement;
2. was rightfully known to the receiving Party without restriction prior to disclosure;
3. is rightfully obtained from a third party without breach of any confidentiality obligation; or
4. is independently developed by the receiving Party without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

## 5. Term and Termination

### 5.1 Term

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until all Order Forms have expired or been terminated.

### 5.2 Termination for Cause

Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after receipt of written notice.

### 5.3 Effect of Termination

Upon termination, Customer's right to access the Services will cease, and each Party will promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information of the other Party.

## 6. Limitation of Liability

EXCEPT FOR LIABILITIES ARISING FROM A PARTY'S BREACH OF ITS CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER TO PROVIDER UNDER THE APPLICABLE ORDER FORM IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

## 7. General

### 7.1 Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

### 7.2 Notices

All notices must be in writing and delivered to the address set forth on the signature page or such other address as a Party may designate by written notice.

### 7.3 Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with all Order Forms, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous communications.
