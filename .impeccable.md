## Design Context

### Users

Lash is for writers, product builders, and small collaborative teams drafting idea documents, plans, and launch notes together. They need to move quickly from blank page to structured thinking, then invite collaborators without losing the thread of the writing.

### Brand Personality

Calm, precise, collaborative. The interface should feel like a serious writing room: quiet by default, fast under the hands, and explicit about where comments, history, AI edits, and routed insights belong.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use Quip as the primary interaction reference: dark global navigation, flat white document canvas, quiet right rail, document-attached outline, restrained coral accents, and low-friction writing controls. Lash should feel more modern and faster than Quip, but not more decorative. Avoid dashboard chrome, nested cards, heavy shadows, marketing layouts, and generic AI gradients.

### Design Principles

1. Writing is the center; chrome earns its space only when it helps navigation, collaboration, or trust.
2. Collaboration feels attached to exact document context, not like a separate admin console.
3. Performance and visual calm are part of the same UX promise: no lag, no unnecessary motion, no layout surprise.
4. The product should disclose power gradually: outline, comments, tables, AI, routing, and sharing stay available without crowding plain drafting.
5. Every deploy or integration affordance must fail loud and state what is unavailable, rather than implying a working path that is not wired.
